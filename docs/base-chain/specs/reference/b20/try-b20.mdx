Explore what B20 can do. Each guide walks through one outcome end to end.

## Tokenize a Real-World Asset

- [Create an Asset Token](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/create-an-asset-token) — Bring a real-world asset onchain as a token you control.
- [Issue Units to Holders](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/issue-units) — Issue units to multiple investors in a single operation.
- [Restrict Eligible Holders](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/restrict-eligible-holders) — Limit ownership to an approved set of investors.
- [Cancel Units](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/cancel-blocked-units) — Cancel units held by an account that is no longer permitted to hold them.
- [Distribute to Holders](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/announce-a-distribution) — Announce and deliver a distribution to holders in one transaction.
- [Adjust Balances with a Multiplier](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/apply-a-multiplier) — Rescale every holder's balance at once, such as a stock split.
- [Pause Transfers](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/pause-transfers) — Halt transfers during an incident while keeping control of supply.

## Issue a Stablecoin

- [Create a Stablecoin](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/issue-your-stablecoin) — Launch a fiat-backed stablecoin onchain.
- [Mint Supply](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/mint-supply) — Create new supply as reserves grow.
- [Burn Supply](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/burn-supply) — Remove supply from circulation on redemption.
- [Restrict Eligible Holders](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/restrict-who-can-hold) — Limit holders to KYC-approved accounts.
- [Block an Account](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/block-an-account) — Freeze a single account without affecting others.
- [Recover Funds from an Account](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/recover-funds) — Reclaim and reissue funds from a lost or compromised account.
- [Add Memos for Reconciliation](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/reconcile-with-memos) — Tag operations with an onchain reference to match offchain records.
- [Pause Token Activity](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/pause-activity) — Halt transfers, mints, or burns independently, then resume.
