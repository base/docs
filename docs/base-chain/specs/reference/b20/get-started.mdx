## What Can I Do With B20?

### Tokenize Assets

- [Create an Asset Token](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/create-an-asset-token) — You want to bring a real-world asset onchain as a token you control.
- [Issue Units to Holders](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/issue-units) — You need to get units into many investors' wallets at once, not one by one.
- [Restrict Eligible Holders](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/restrict-eligible-holders) — You need to make sure only approved investors can ever hold the asset.
- [Cancel Blocked Units](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/cancel-blocked-units) — You need to claw back units from someone no longer allowed to hold them.
- [Announce a Distribution](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/announce-a-distribution) — You want to notify holders of a payout and deliver it in the same step.
- [Apply a Multiplier](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/apply-a-multiplier) — You need to adjust everyone's balance at once, like a stock split, without touching individual wallets.
- [Pause Transfers](/build-on-base/issue-rwa/pause-transfers) — You need to freeze trading during an incident while keeping control of supply.

### Issue a Stablecoin

- [Issue Your Stablecoin](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/issue-your-stablecoin) — You want to launch your own fiat-backed stablecoin onchain.
- [Mint Supply](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/mint-supply) — You need to create new coins as real-world reserves come in.
- [Burn Supply](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/burn-supply) — You need to remove coins from circulation when customers cash out.
- [Restrict Who Can Hold It](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/restrict-who-can-hold) — You need to keep your coin with KYC-approved users only.
- [Block an Account](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/block-an-account) — You need to freeze one bad actor without affecting everyone else.
- [Recover Funds](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/recover-funds) — You need to get funds back from a lost or compromised account.
- [Pause Activity](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/pause-activity) — You need to stop everything fast during an incident, then turn it back on.
- [Reconcile with Memos](/build-on-base/issue-stablecoins/reconcile-with-memos) — You need to match onchain activity to your offchain books at scale.
