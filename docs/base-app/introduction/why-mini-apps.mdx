# Why Mini Apps

The next evolution of digital experiences extends beyond app stores into social feeds and direct messages. Mini Apps are lightweight, social-native apps that combine traditional discoverability through top and featured app listings with social distribution directly in your feed. They launch instantly when you tap them: no downloads, no friction, just immediate engagement and distribution.


<Note>
Existing web applications can be converted into Mini Apps. Learn how to [integrate your existing app](/base-app/build-with-minikit/existing-app-integration) with our helpful guide.
</Note>

## Beyond the App Store Model

Traditional apps face costly user acquisition because discovery is limited to app store interfaces, followed by download friction. Mini Apps flip this dynamic by bringing your app directly to where users already spend their time: their social feeds.

## What Makes Mini Apps Different

### For Users: Frictionless Discovery and Engagement

Mini Apps eliminate the gap between discovery and engagement. Instead of downloading apps you might never use again, you can instantly try interactive experiences shared by friends.

**Key benefits for users:**
- Instant access: No downloads or installation required
- Social context: See who else is participating and how you compare
- Reduced commitment: Try experiences in just one click.

### For Builders: Built-in Social Infrastructure

As a developer, you build on top of existing social infrastructure instead of recreating it from scratch.

**What you get out of the box:**
- User identity and authentication
- Social connections and friend graphs
- Viral distribution mechanisms
- Immediate access to engaged communities

<Tip>
When someone shares a Mini App, they're sharing the entire interactive experience, not just a link.
</Tip>

## The Network Effect Advantage

<Frame caption="Base App Fly wheel">
<img src="/images/minikit/flywheel.png" alt="Image of builder flywheel" />
</Frame>

When users engage with Mini Apps, their activity fuels discovery for other apps, creating a powerful flywheel effect:

1. **More engaged users** attract more builders to the platform
2. **More builders** create better, more diverse experiences  
3. **Better experiences** drive even more user activity
4. **Platform growth tools** amplify this cycle for all builders

<Check>
The result: exponential growth that lifts all apps together, with every builder benefiting from the collective network effect.
</Check>

## From Idea to Live Application

The development path is streamlined and permissionless:

<Steps>
<Step title="Build your Mini App">
  Use [MiniKit](/base-app/build-with-minikit/quickstart) or the [Farcaster SDK](https://miniapps.farcaster.xyz/docs/getting-started) to create your application.
  
  <Info>
  No special permissions or approval processes required to show up in the Base App.
  </Info>
</Step>

<Step title="Deploy directly">
  Deploy your Mini App without waiting for approval processes or store reviews.
  
  <Check>
  Test your application immediately with real users.
  </Check>
</Step>

<Step title="Get discovered automatically">
  Post your Mini App to Base App and it gets automatically indexed for discovery.
  
  Your app becomes instantly discoverable in:
  - Base App search results
  - The broader Farcaster ecosystem
  - User social feeds through organic sharing
</Step>

<Step title="Iterate based on real usage">
  Monitor actual usage patterns and iterate based on real user feedback rather than building in isolation.
</Step>
</Steps>
