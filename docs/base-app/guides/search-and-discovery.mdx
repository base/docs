## How Users Discover Your Mini App

The Base app offers multiple discovery surfaces designed to help users find exciting onchain apps and Mini Apps. Understanding these surfaces helps you optimize your Mini App for better discoverability and user engagement.

### Primary Discovery Surfaces

#### Search
<Frame caption="Here in The Base App you can see searching based on partial titles">
<img src="/images/minikit/search.jpg" alt="search bar in base app" height="200"/>
</Frame>

**Purpose:** Users discover Mini Apps through direct search queries.

Your Mini App will only appear in search results after it has been indexed. To ensure indexing, share your Mini App at least once in Base App. Indexing typically takes ~10 (approx) minutes after the first share.

**Managing Search Indexing:**

**Development Environment:**
Add `"noindex": true` to your manifest to prevent development versions from appearing in search results:

```json
{
  "frame": {
    "noindex": true,
    "name": "Your App (Dev)",
    // ... other fields
  }
}
```

**Removing from Search:**
To remove your Mini App from search results:

- **Invalidate manifest:** Update your manifest to make it invalid (removes from all discovery).

<Warning>
If your Mini App does not show in search please follow the debugging guide [here](/base-app/build-with-minikit/debugging#1-app-discovery--indexing-issues)
</Warning>


#### Social Feed Discovery

<Frame caption="Mini App in the feed">
<img src="/images/minikit/feed_mini.jpg" alt="mini app feed" height="200"/>
</Frame>

**Purpose:** Users discover Mini Apps through shared posts and search.

When users share Mini App links in social feeds, rich embeds appear that allow direct access to your Mini App.

**Technical implementation:**
```html
<meta name="fc:frame" content='{"version":"next","imageUrl":"https://your-app.com/embed-image","button":{"title":"Play Now","action":{"type":"launch_frame","name":"Your App","url":"https://your-app.com"}}}' />
```

**Best practices:**
- Add `fc:frame` meta tags to make pages shareable as embeds
- Optimize embed images for 3:2 aspect ratio
- Include compelling call-to-action text in embed buttons
- Use dynamic embed generation for personalized content

<Warning>
If your Mini App does not show as an embed please follow the debugging guide [here](/base-app/build-with-minikit/debugging#3-embed-rendering-issues)
</Warning>

**Purpose:** Personal launcher and quick access hub for users' favorite apps

## Optimization Strategies

### Build for Discovery

1. **Implement proper manifest files:** Ensure your `/.well-known/farcaster.json` includes all required metadata including proper categorization. [Example here](base-app/introduction/mini-apps#example-manifest)

2. **Choose relevant categories:** Select the most appropriate primary category in your manifest to appear in the right discovery sections

3. **Create shareable moments:** Build features that naturally encourage users to share their achievements, creations, or experiences. Follow our [Thinking Social Guide](/base-app/guides/thinking-social)

4. **Optimize for virality:** Include social mechanics that reward sharing and inviting friends, such as:
   - Achievement sharing
   - Collaborative features
   - Referral rewards
   - Social leaderboards

5. **Design compelling embeds:** Create eye-catching embed images and clear value propositions that encourage clicks from social feeds

6. **Encourage saves:** Implement prompts for users to save your Mini App to their personal launcher for easy return access.

<Tip>
You can prompt users to save your Mini App using the [useAddFrame hook](/base-app/build-with-minikit/overview#useaddframe). Consider waiting until users experience value - after completing their first action or reaching a milestone - for better conversion rates.
</Tip>
