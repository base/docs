Base backs builders at every stage. This is your directory for the support to help you ship faster, grow faster, and build onchain.

Find your stage below to see what that support looks like.

## Learn

- **Tutorials:** [Learn the fundamentals](/get-started/base) that will help you get started with building onchain.

- **Builder Quests:** Want to pick up a problem statement to apply your learnings?

  Builder Quests are Base's recurring challenges to the builder community: pick a theme, build something real around it, and get rewarded for it.

  Look out for Quests on [Build on Base](https://x.com/buildonbase).

## Build

- **Builder Communities:** Find fellow builders to learn and build alongside. Builder communities serve as independent support groups for builders on Base. Join them and share your ideas, builds, provide and seek feedback.

  Base-aligned builder communities:

  1. [Home Base](https://x.com/homebasedotlove) (South America)
  2. [Inner Circle](https://x.com/innercircle_so) (India and globally)

  Running a community and want to be featured? [Fill out this form](https://forms.gle/wduas5Sg4wERD51V7).

- **Office Hours:** The Base team opens office hours every Friday for builders looking for feedback on product or GTM.

  Book your slot here: [Office Hours with the Base team](https://forms.gle/iG1oWfGkuMPAp8HX7).

## Accelerate

- **Builder Codes:** The Base team provides grants very selectively. The first step to be eligible for grants is to integrate [Builder Codes](/specifications/builder-codes/overview).

  These are unique identifiers that tag your app's transactions so Base can attribute onchain activity back to you — and we wanted to make sure you're set up.

- **Builder Grant Program:** Get up to $5,000 in grants, GTM and product support for actively building on Base across:

  1. Prediction markets
  2. Token launchpads
  3. DeFi 2.0 — lending, borrowing, looping, yield generation, vaults, tokenized equities
  4. Agents — commerce, x402 implementations, autonomous trading
  5. Products focusing on new asset creation
  6. Consumer apps focused on transaction growth

  [Apply here](https://forms.gle/urqjBJYJjR6Vqqre6) to be included in our next cohort.

- **Creator Grant Program:** We're backing independent creators with up to $4,000 who are already making great content around Base across:

  1. Writers covering Base (deep dives, memes, analysis, threads)
  2. Streamers and live-show hosts
  3. Creators running a recurring podcast or show
  4. Independent video creators
  5. Educators covering Base in their own language

  [Apply here](https://forms.gle/sZ5cBADdXLfbbe1R8) to be included in our next cohort.

- **Builder Stack:** The [Builder Stack](/get-started/base-services-hub) packages this. A pool of tools and credits, spanning LLM inference, cloud, and other dev infrastructure, that a team unlocks once it's accepted into the Builder Grant Program.

- **Accelerator Partners:** We back builders beyond our own programs through third-party accelerators run by our partners.

  1. [Circuit Accelerator](https://www.circuit-accelerator.com/): A four-week accelerator connecting founders and investors in the heart of Southeast Asia.

- **Distribution:** If you're actively building on Base with traction, we'd love to support you on our socials.

  [Book an office hours slot](https://forms.gle/iG1oWfGkuMPAp8HX7) with the Base team to talk about what you're building.

## Scale

- **Base Batches:** [Base Batches](/get-started/base-batches) is an accelerator for early-stage teams building the future of finance on Base, run by the Base Ecosystem Fund. Each cohort is a short, virtual program that ends with a demo day in front of investors.

  Learn more on the [program page](https://www.base.org/batches).

- **Base Ecosystem Fund:** The [Base Ecosystem Fund](/get-started/base-ecosystem-fund) is the strategic investment arm of Base, run in partnership with Coinbase Ventures. It backs early-stage teams building onchain businesses on Base.

  Learn more on the [fund page](https://www.base.org/ecosystem-fund).
