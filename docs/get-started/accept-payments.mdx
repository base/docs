Accept commerce payments on Base with onchain controls for delayed settlement, partial fulfillment, cancellation, payer recovery, bounded fees, and refunds. The Commerce Payments Protocol provides two checkout paths: immediate `charge`, or escrow-backed `authorize` followed by `capture`.

## Choose the Checkout Path

| Requirement | Start with |
|---|---|
| The order can settle immediately | [Charge a payment](/build-on-base/accept-payments/charge-a-payment) |
| Fulfillment, tax, inventory, or the final amount is decided later | [Authorize a payment](/build-on-base/accept-payments/authorize-a-payment) |
| The order ships or settles in increments | [Capture a partial amount](/build-on-base/accept-payments/capture-a-partial-amount) |

Both paths create a unique onchain payment identified by `paymentInfoHash`. The payer approves immutable parties, token, maximum amount, expiry windows, and fee bounds before an operator advances the lifecycle.

## Payment Lifecycle

  <img src="/images/commerce-payments/payment-lifecycle.png" alt="Commerce Payments Protocol lifecycle from charge or authorization through capture, void, reclaim, and refund" />

| Stage | What happens | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Configure | Define the operator, payer, receiver, token, maximum, expiries, fees, and unique salt | [Configure a payment](/build-on-base/accept-payments/configure-a-payment) |
| Collect | Convert a wallet approval into an exact token transfer | [Choose a token collector](/build-on-base/accept-payments/choose-a-token-collector) |
| Charge or authorize | Settle immediately, or reserve funds in escrow | [Commerce Payments Protocol](/build-on-base/accept-payments/use-commerce-payments-protocol) |
| Capture | Transfer all or part of an authorization to the receiver and fee receiver | [Capture an authorization](/build-on-base/accept-payments/capture-an-authorization) |
| Cancel or recover | The operator voids, or the payer reclaims after authorization expiry | [Void](/build-on-base/accept-payments/void-an-authorization) and [reclaim](/build-on-base/accept-payments/reclaim-an-authorization) |
| Refund | Source replacement liquidity and return captured value to the original payer | [Refund a payment](/build-on-base/accept-payments/refund-a-payment) |
