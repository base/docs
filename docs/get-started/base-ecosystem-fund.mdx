The Base Ecosystem Fund is the strategic investment arm of Base, run in partnership with Coinbase Ventures. It backs early-stage teams building onchain businesses on Base. Learn more on the [fund page](https://www.base.org/ecosystem-fund).

## What It Offers

- Pre-seed and seed investment for teams building on Base.
- Hands-on ecosystem support and partnership introductions.
- Partner credits from providers such as AWS, Azure, and Alchemy.
- Priority access to Coinbase Prime, Coinbase Business, and onramp APIs.

## Who It's For

Pre-seed and seed founders building enduring onchain primitives and businesses that drive real economic activity, across:

- Trading
- Payments
- AI agents
- Other onchain businesses
