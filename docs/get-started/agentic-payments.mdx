Accept dollars on Base. USDC is a fully-backed digital dollar that settles in under two seconds for pennies in gas — no card networks, no FX fees, no chargebacks, and no processor cut. [Base Pay](/sdks/base-account/reference/base-pay/pay) turns that into one-tap checkout, recurring subscriptions, and machine-to-machine payments, reaching every Base Account and Coinbase user out of the box.
